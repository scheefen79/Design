📖 Metrics Definitions

Definitions for every metric used in the dashboard, grouped by the part of the pipeline they evaluate.

Term translation

Metrics that score how well glossary terms are translated, both at utterance and at conversation level.

Term Accuracy (Retriever)
Exact / semantic term match accuracy.
↑ higher is betterRange: 0–1
Term Accuracy (Morphological)
Fuzzy morphological match accuracy.
↑ higher is betterRange: 0–1
Domain Consistency
Consistency of term usage across the conversation.
↑ higher is betterRange: 0–1
Conv. Accuracy (Retriever)
Conversation-level retriever accuracy.
↑ higher is betterRange: 0–1
Conv. Accuracy (Morphological)
Conversation-level morphological accuracy.
↑ higher is betterRange: 0–1
Term transcription

Metrics that score how accurately the speech-to-text layer captures glossary-relevant content.

Domain-Specific WER
Word Error Rate on utterances containing glossary source terms.
↓ lower is betterRange: 0–1+
